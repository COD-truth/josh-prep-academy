# Josh & Co first release

The first release is the assisted WhatsApp flow. It does not automatically reserve a slot or collect a payment. Later booking and lesson features remain separate.

## Launch checks

1. Confirm the WhatsApp number already published by Josh & Co, +237 691 916 747. The request page opens WhatsApp with a draft; nothing is sent automatically.
2. Publish up to five approved profiles with photo, subjects, levels and rate. Use the existing tutor directory administration once its database migration and permissions are in place. The request page works even when the catalogue is unavailable.
3. Confirm the receiving names and numbers for Orange Money and MTN MoMo. The public request page tells families to wait for official instructions. The team shares the amount and payment details after agreeing on the lesson and verifies payment manually.
4. For online classes, share the specific Meet or Classroom link with the family privately.
5. On the preview, try a French and an English request, a profile-specific request, and the mobile navigation. Confirm that WhatsApp opens with the right contact and a readable draft. Use a test request agreed with the client before sharing the production site.
6. Check that the chosen hosting target is healthy and the catalogue has real approved profiles before marking the first release accepted. See OWNER_REQUIREMENTS.md for database and hosting setup.

## Acceptance

A parent can find a published tutor, open the request form without signing in, and reach the correct team contact with the right subject, level, format and optional tutor preference. Staff can explain price, timing, payment and online access. No automatic payment or booking is claimed.
